EasyAptHow it works

Find the exact apartment. The unit, the floor, the price.

Type an area. EasyApt reads each apartment community's own website right now and shows every unit that fits, down to the floor it's on.

  1. Search an area

    Any US city, neighborhood, or ZIP code. We find the apartment communities there.

  2. We check their websites live

    Prices and open units come from each property's own site at the moment you search, not a listing posted weeks ago.

  3. Pick your unit

    Compare floor plans, see which units are open and what floor they're on, and walk through the 3D tour.

Some properties don't publish availability online. We still list them with a link to their website, so none get left out. A first search in a new area takes up to a minute.
